About Pragati Telecom

Empowering rural computer centers, cyber cafe operators, students, and citizens with high-speed, browser-based digital utilities.

📍 Kalupur Middle Para, Murshidabad 📅 Established for Local Digital Empowerment

🌐 Who We Are

Pragati Telecom is an independent digital service and utility platform founded in Kalupur Middle Para, Murshidabad, West Bengal. Originating as a local customer service and digital facilitation point, Pragati Telecom developed this online suite of web-based utilities to solve everyday formatting, printing, cropping, and document management hurdles faced by local operators and individuals.

In modern digital workflows, computer operators and citizens frequently deal with strict upload guidelines for government exams, scholarship forms, recruitment portals, and PVC printing machines. Standard software is often bloated, requires paid desktop licenses, or uploads sensitive user documents to unfamiliar cloud servers. Pragati Telecom provides lightweight, client-side, browser-native tools that execute swiftly without software installations.

🛠️ Our Digital Tool Categories

The Pragati Telecom portal offers a specialized collection of browser-based tools categorized into four practical areas:

  • 📸 Passport Photo Studio & AI Editing: Instant standard 35×45mm passport and 2×2 inch visa cropping, client-side background removal via WebAssembly neural models, photo enhancement filters, and instant 4×6 inch and A4 photo tiling for direct color printing.
  • 💳 Smart Multi-Card Formatting: Intelligent document structure analysis for official document formats (such as Aadhaar, PAN, Ayushman PMJAY, and Ration Cards), automatic side-by-side front and back alignment, PDF password decryption prompts, and precise 85.6×54mm PVC tray printing layouts.
  • 📄 High-Precision PDF Suite: Including our Custom PDF Compressor (which uses intelligent binary search to reach exact target KB limits like 50KB or 200KB for portal uploads), Smart Visual PDF Editor (with Google Noto Sans Bengali font embedding), and Drag & Drop PDF Maker.
  • 🔎 Multi-Column OCR & Searchable PDF: Transforming scanned land records, Bengali electoral voter rolls, and multi-column tabular documents into 100% searchable, selectable text PDFs.
  • 💵 Business Utilities: Instant daily cash denomination tally calculator designed specifically for evening retail and counter cash closings.

🎯 Who Can Benefit from Pragati Telecom?

Our web tools are crafted with simplicity and speed to assist diverse user groups:

  • CSC & Digital Seva Kendra Operators: Accelerating daily card printouts, photo printing, and customer document resizing.
  • Cyber Cafe & Online Service Centers: Streamlining multi-page scanning, compression, and student application uploads.
  • Job Aspirants & Students: Cropping signature and passport photos to exact millimeter and kilobyte specifications mandated by job portals.
  • General Citizens: Managing family identity documents, merging multi-page land records, and generating clean printable layouts from home.

🔒 Privacy-First Engineering

We strongly prioritize digital privacy. Personal identity cards, photographs, and certificates should not be needlessly harvested by third-party servers. For this reason:

  • 90%+ of our tools operate 100% locally: Processing occurs strictly within your browser’s temporary memory (RAM) using HTML5 Canvas, Web Workers, and JavaScript libraries.
  • No Server Uploads: Files processed in our Photo Studio, Smart Card Studio, PDF Compressor, and PDF Editor never leave your personal computer or mobile phone.
  • Opt-In AI Features: When using advanced AI OCR in our Searchable PDF tool, users explicitly supply their own Google Gemini API key, ensuring direct communication between the client and Google's official API endpoint without third-party middleman interception.
