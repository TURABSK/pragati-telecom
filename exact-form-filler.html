📄 How Exact Form Filler Pro Works

Traditional PDF form filling tools often attempt to re-generate or recreate documents from scratch, leading to shifted lines, altered watermarks, and font distortions that fail government portal submissions. Exact Form Filler Pro employs an immutable dual-layer architecture:

🔒

Immutable Original Base

The uploaded PDF or high-resolution scan is preserved as the untouchable base layer. Every original watermark, logo, border dot, and instruction line remains 100% intact.

🎯

Normalized Coordinate Mapping

Coordinates are stored as resolution-independent decimal fractions (0.0 to 1.0) relative to the physical PDF page dimensions, guaranteeing pixel-perfect alignment across all zooms.

🔤

Character & Digit Box Grids

Automatically calculates box pitch and centers individual letters or numbers inside government form grids (e.g. [T][U][R][A][B] or 12-digit Aadhaar boxes).

🖨️ Physical Printer Accuracy & Calibration Tips

While the digital PDF output preserves exact point dimensions, physical desktop printers (Canon, Epson, HP, Brother) may have slight 1–2 mm mechanical feed variances. Follow these guidelines for flawless alignment:

  • Select "Actual Size (100%)": In your browser print dialog, always disable "Fit to Printable Area" or "Shrink to Fit".
  • Use Hardware Calibration Offsets: Click the 🖨️ Printer Calibration button in the top bar to set a global horizontal or vertical offset (in millimeters) to adjust for your specific printer's mechanical feed.
  • Photo & Signature Transparency: When uploading customer signatures, our built-in thresholding removes paper background whitening so the signature ink sits naturally over printed form lines.

🔒 Customer Data Privacy & Local Storage Guarantee

100% Client-Side In-Browser Processing: All PDF parsing, canvas rendering, character grid calculations, and PDF-Lib font embeddings execute strictly inside your device's browser memory (RAM). Customer identity information, photos, and signatures are never uploaded to or stored on Pragati Telecom servers.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Exact Form Filler)

Will the original form watermark, logo, or stamp be removed?
No. The original document is completely immutable. User data is rendered strictly as an overlay layer on top of your existing artwork.
Can I write Bengali or Hindi names on the form?
Yes. Exact Form Filler Pro embeds official Google Noto Sans Bengali and Noto Sans Devanagari Unicode font subsets into the generated PDF file.
Can I save a calibrated form layout to reuse for multiple customers?
Yes. Click "Save Template" to store the field layout in your browser, or export it as a JSON file to share across your cyber café workstations.
How does Character Box mode work for applicant names?
Character Box mode distributes each typed letter into separate square boxes with configurable width and gap spacing, centering each letter automatically.
