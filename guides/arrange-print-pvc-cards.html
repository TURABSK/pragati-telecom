How to Arrange and Print PVC Identity Cards (CR80 Standard)

Printing double-sided physical PVC plastic cards or wallet-sized laminated prints is one of the most common daily services at local cyber cafes and CSC centers. Here is how to format dimensions, handle password decryption, and ensure flawless printer alignment.

The CR80 Standard: Exact PVC Card Dimensions

Standard plastic identity cards, driving licenses, ATM cards, and health cards globally follow the ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 (CR80) form factor:

  • Physical Width: 85.60 mm (3.370 inches)
  • Physical Height: 53.98 mm (2.125 inches)
  • Corner Radius: 3.18 mm (rounded corners)
  • Digital Resolution @ 300 DPI: Exactly 1011 × 638 pixels

Handling Encrypted e-Aadhaar & Identity PDFs

When customers download electronic identity documents (like e-Aadhaar), the PDF is encrypted by the issuing authority. The universal password structure for e-Aadhaar is:

Password Formula: [First 4 letters of Name in CAPITAL] + [Year of Birth (YYYY)]
Example: Name "SURESH KUMAR", Birth Year 1992 → Password is SURE1992

In Smart Multi-Card Studio, the password prompt operates entirely within your browser's PDF.js sandbox, decrypting the vector image locally without ever uploading the password across the network.

Two Common PVC Printing Workflows

Workflow A: Direct Inkjet PVC Card Trays (Epson / Canon)

If your center uses an inkjet printer with an official PVC card tray (e.g. Epson L805, L850, L8050 or Canon G570):

  1. Place two blank printable inkjet PVC cards in Tray Slot 1 (Front) and Slot 2 (Back).
  2. The software arranges the front and back card images with 8mm horizontal spacing to match standard dual-slot tray templates.
  3. In the print dialog, select Media Type: "CD/DVD or Card Tray" and set Scaling to 100%.

Workflow B: Glossy Photo Paper + Pouch Lamination

If you do not have a dedicated card tray:

  1. Print the side-by-side layout on 180–230 GSM glossy photo paper.
  2. Cut along the outer 1px black border using a paper cutter or scissors.
  3. Fold the front and back faces together along the center dividing line.
  4. Insert into an 80–125 micron thermal PVC pouch and pass through your hot laminator at 110°C–130°C.

Critical Print Setting: Avoid "Fit to Printable Area"

The number one reason for incorrectly sized card prints is the browser's print dialog scaling setting:

  • ❌ "Fit to Page" or "Fit to Printable Area": The browser artificially shrinks the layout by 5% to 8% to fit arbitrary margin rules, resulting in a card smaller than standard CR80 dimensions.
  • ✅ "Actual Size" or "Scale: 100%": Keeps millimeter dimensions exact to the millimeter.
