How to Prepare Passport and ID Photos: Dimensions, Crop & Print Layouts

Whether you are running a busy cyber cafe studio counter or applying for an online recruitment portal, formatting customer portrait photos correctly is essential to avoid application rejection.

⚡ Quick Summary

The most common photo standard for Indian recruitment portals and official identity cards is 35 mm width × 45 mm height at 300 DPI (413 × 531 pixels), with the face covering roughly 70–80% of the vertical frame. Framing & Facial Composition Guidelines

A great passport photo requires proper framing:

  • Face Coverage (70–80%): The distance from the bottom of the chin to the top of the head should occupy roughly 70% to 80% of the photo's total vertical height.
  • Eye Level: Eyes should be positioned horizontally level, roughly two-thirds of the way up the image.
  • Shoulder Balance: Both shoulders should be equally visible with the subject looking straight into the camera lens.
  • Neutral Expression: Neutral facial expression with both ears visible and eyes open without hair covering the face.

Background Selection: White vs. Light Blue

Most modern Indian recruitment portals (such as Staff Selection Commission and Railway Recruitment Boards) specifically mandate a plain, light-colored background—predominantly pure white or light sky blue.

Taking photos against uneven home curtains or dark walls creates shadows that can fail automated validation checks. Using AI Photo Studio Pro's client-side background removal instantly isolates the person and fills the background with formal pure white (#ffffff) or soft blue (#dbeafe).

Printing Multiple Photos on 4×6 Inch Photo Sheets

Printing individual 35×45mm photos one by one on large paper wastes expensive photo sheets. Standard photographic paper comes in 4×6 inch (102 × 152 mm) size:

  1. At 35×45mm dimensions with 2mm cutting gaps, a 4×6 inch sheet fits exactly 8 passport photos arranged in a 2-column by 4-row grid.
  2. On an A4 document sheet (210 × 297 mm), you can fit up to 30–36 passport photos for batch student registrations.
  3. Adding a thin 1px black cutting border (stroke) makes it much easier to cut each picture straight using scissors or a photo die-cutter.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Selfie Distortions: Taking close-up selfies with a front mobile camera causes wide-angle facial distortion (large nose, recessed ears). Take the photo from at least 4–5 feet away with a 2× telephoto or standard rear camera.
  • Heavy Digital Filters: Never apply beauty smoothing or artificial color filters. Skin tones should look natural.
  • Glossy Reflections on Eyeglasses: Heavy flash glare covering the applicant's pupils is a common reason for rejection. Tilt glasses slightly downward or photograph without glasses if permitted.

Frequently Asked Questions

What printer settings should I use for 4×6 photo paper?
In your printer dialog (Epson, Canon, HP), select Paper Size: 4×6 in (10×15 cm), Paper Type: Glossy Photo Paper, Print Quality: High, and Scaling: 100% (Actual Size) to avoid distortion.
Why is 300 DPI resolution necessary for physical prints?
Standard computer displays show 72 to 96 dots per inch, but physical inkjet photo printers require 300 dots per inch (DPI) to avoid visible pixelation and ensure sharp facial details.
