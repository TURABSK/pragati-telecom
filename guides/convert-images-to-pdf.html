How to Convert Multiple Images into a Single Multi-Page PDF

When submitting job applications, project dossiers, or expense receipts, sending ten separate image files is messy and often prohibited. Here is how to assemble images and PDF pages into a single organized, compact document.

⚡ Quick Summary

To create a single PDF from multiple photos (JPG, PNG, WebP) or existing PDFs: open Drag & Drop PDF Maker, select your files, reorder pages by dragging thumbnails in the workspace grid, choose your target size (e.g. 500 KB or 1000 KB), and click Generate Merged PDF.

Why Combining Images into a Single PDF Is Better

Submitting loose photos of multi-page documents creates multiple problems:

  • Scattered Order: Photo names like IMG_20260830_001.jpg frequently get uploaded out of sequence on recruitment portals.
  • Huge Total File Size: Modern smartphone cameras produce 4 MB to 12 MB images per photo. Ten photos equal 80 MB, easily crashing upload forms that accept a 2 MB or 5 MB maximum.
  • Universal Compatibility: A single PDF document opens identically across Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS devices with consistent page numbering.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select Files: Click "Select Images / PDF Pages" to upload your photos (JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP) or existing PDF documents.
  2. Reorder Pages: The workspace grid generates visual thumbnail cards for every page. Click and drag any card to re-arrange pages into the correct sequential order.
  3. Remove Unneeded Pages: Click the red ✕ button on any thumbnail to discard blurred or accidental snapshots.
  4. Add Additional Files: Click "+ Add More Files" if you need to insert additional documents into the existing workspace.
  5. Adjust Target File Size: Set the Target Size slider (e.g. 500 KB or 1000 KB) depending on your portal's requirements.
  6. Generate & Download: Click "Generate Merged PDF". The in-browser engine applies multi-pass compression and creates your finalized file.

Mixing Portrait & Landscape Orientations

Documents often combine vertical portrait pages (such as marksheets and bio-data) with horizontal landscape pages (such as land maps or tabular bank statements).

Our PDF maker dynamically inspects the aspect ratio of each uploaded page individually (first.w > first.h ? 'landscape' : 'portrait'), ensuring landscape photos are not squished or rotated sideways into vertical pages.

Local Standard Mode vs. Optional AI OCR

  • Standard Mode (OCR Unchecked): Operates 100% offline inside your browser RAM. Ideal for standard image-to-PDF merging and compression.
  • AI OCR Mode (OCR Checked): Automatically embeds an invisible, selectable Bengali and English text layer behind the photos via our secure Cloudflare Worker server-side AI. No personal API key is required.

🔒 Privacy & Data Security Notice

Complete Local Privacy by Default: In standard mode, all image resizing, canvas rendering, and PDF generation occur in your browser memory. No images are uploaded to Pragati Telecom servers.
