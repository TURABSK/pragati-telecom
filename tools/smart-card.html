💳 How Smart Multi-Card Studio Works

Smart Multi-Card Studio is an automated document formatting and print-alignment utility engineered for cyber cafes, CSC centers, and digital service operators. When customers bring password-protected electronic identity PDFs or photographed cards, this tool handles decryption prompts, auto-detects the document type (Aadhaar, PAN, Ayushman PMJAY, e-Shram, Ration Card), isolates the front and back card regions, and positions them side-by-side in exact 85.6 × 54 mm standard PVC dimensions.

🔓

Password-Protected PDF Decryption

Prompts for the document password directly in the browser and decrypts the vector canvas locally without transmitting password data to any server.

✂️

Intelligent Boundary Extraction

Includes specialized coordinate extractors for Aadhaar (bottom half), e-Shram (dual card stack), PAN cards, Ayushman Bharat, and dual-column Ration cards.

🖨️

Side-by-Side PVC & A4 Print

Automatically arranges front and back card faces side-by-side with 8mm spacing, ready for PVC tray printers or laminated A4 paper printing.

📋 Step-by-Step Operating Instructions

  1. Upload Document: Drag and drop your electronic PDF or scanned image (JPG, PNG) into the upload box.
  2. Password Unlock (If Protected): If your PDF is locked (e.g. e-Aadhaar), enter the password (e.g. first 4 letters of name in CAPITAL + year of birth) into the browser prompt.
  3. Auto-Detection: The engine automatically classifies the card type and crops the front and back sections.
  4. Fine-Tune Alignment: Use the Zoom (↔/↕) and Position (Left/Top) sliders if you wish to adjust margins or crop lines.
  5. Print or Export: Click "Direct Print" (Ctrl+P) to print directly, "JPG সেভ (PVC সাইজ)" for standard 85.6×54 mm (300 DPI) cards, "পাশাপাশি JPG" for tray/folding print sheet, or "PDF সেভ (PVC রেডি)" for 100% actual-size vector PDF.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Multi-Card Studio)

Why does the browser ask for a password when opening e-Aadhaar?
Official e-Aadhaar PDF files from UIDAI are encrypted. The password is required to decrypt the document. Smart Multi-Card Studio decrypts the PDF directly inside your browser memory using PDF.js and never uploads your password.
Will the printed card match standard PVC card tray dimensions?
Yes. The card bounding box is locked to 85.6 × 54 mm (CR80 standard), which corresponds exactly to standard PVC plastic cards and Epson/Canon card trays.
Can I process multiple cards at the same time?
Yes. You can upload multiple PDFs or card photos together. They will be added sequentially onto the A4 page for batch printing.
How do I print both front and back on a single PVC card?
The tool places the front and back side-by-side with 8mm spacing. If you use an inkjet card tray, place card blanks in Tray Slot 1 & Slot 2. If using standard photo paper and a laminator, fold the cut sheet along the center line.
What should I do if auto-crop clips a margin?
You can use the real-time adjustment sliders on the right of each card box (Zoom ↔, Zoom ↕, Left ⇄, Top ⇅) to expand or re-center the crop box before printing.

টুলটির "Card Outline / Stroke (বর্ডার)" প্যানেল অথবা উপরের টুলবারের "🔲 বর্ডার" বোতামে ক্লিক করে এক ক্লিকেই আউটলাইন চালু বা বন্ধ করতে পারেন। ফটো পেপারে প্রিন্ট করে কাঁচি দিয়ে কাটার সুবিধার জন্য বর্ডার চালু রাখুন; আর সরাসরি প্লাস্টিক PVC কার্ড ট্রেতে প্রিন্ট করার জন্য বর্ডার বন্ধ করে দিন।
