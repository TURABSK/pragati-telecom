🗜️ How Custom PDF Compressor Works

Government recruitment boards, college admission portals, and scholarship systems in India commonly impose strict file-size limits (such as "Upload marksheet under 100 KB" or "Certificate between 50 KB and 200 KB"). Traditional compressors merely reduce quality by arbitrary percentages (e.g. low/medium/high), often leaving the file still too large or making text unreadable.

Our Custom PDF Compressor uses a client-side binary search algorithm. It dynamically renders PDF pages at varying resolutions and JPEG compression qualities (through 6 outer scale passes and 6 inner quality tests) to calculate the maximum visual clarity that fits within your exact Target KB ceiling.

⚙️ Technical Limitations & Quality Considerations

To achieve precise kilobyte reductions, the tool renders vector pages into optimized JPEG streams. Please keep the following technical considerations in mind:

  • Scanned Documents & Certificates: Highly effective for scanned marksheets, voter slips, electricity bills, and certificates.
  • Small Target Ceilings: Compressing a multi-page PDF to very small targets (e.g. 30 KB for a 5-page document) will naturally reduce image sharpness to fit within the mathematical byte constraint.
  • Encrypted PDFs: Password-protected PDFs should first be decrypted or printed to a standard PDF before compression.

🔒 Privacy & Local Processing Disclosure

100% Client-Side In-Browser Execution: PDF page parsing (via PDF.js) and document reconstruction (via PDF-Lib) occur exclusively within your device's browser memory (RAM). Your confidential documents are never uploaded to Pragati Telecom servers or third-party cloud services.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (PDF Compressor)

Why did the compressed size end up slightly below my target?
To ensure your document never exceeds the maximum portal limit (e.g. a 200 KB limit failing at 200.5 KB), the binary search algorithm ensures the output size is strictly less than or equal to your chosen Target KB.
Will compressed text remain readable?
For standard target settings (100 KB–300 KB per single page), certificates, signatures, and marksheets remain clear and legible for verification officers.
Can I compress multi-page PDF documents?
Yes. The compressor automatically loops across all pages in the PDF document, applying consistent resolution and compression quality to each page.
Why did the tool tell me my text-based PDF was preserved?
Certain digital PDFs consist purely of compact vector text fonts. Converting clean vector text into image-based JPEG pages can actually increase file size; in such cases, the tool intelligently preserves your original file.
Is there a file upload limit?
Because processing happens in your browser's RAM, documents up to 50 MB with 10–20 pages can be compressed easily on standard computers and smartphones.
