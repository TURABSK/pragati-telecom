📸 How AI Photo Studio Pro Works

AI Photo Studio Pro is a browser-based passport photo cropper and photo printing layout engine. Designed specifically for cyber cafes, studio photographers, and online form applicants, it handles the entire workflow of standardizing customer portraits, removing uneven background shadows, adding formal borders, and arranging multiple copies on standard 4×6 inch glossy paper or A4 sheets.

📐

Standard Aspect Ratios

Built-in presets for 35×45mm passport size, 51×51mm (2×2 inch) visa size, and 25×35mm stamp size, plus custom millimeter inputs.

✂️

Local AI Background Removal

Utilizes client-side machine learning WebAssembly to detect subjects and replace cluttered backgrounds with clean white or light blue.

🖨️

300 DPI Print Tiling

Automatically calculates pixel dimensions at 300 DPI print quality and tiles multiple passport copies with 2mm cutting gaps.

📋 Step-by-Step Usage Guide

  1. Upload Portrait: Click the upload zone and select any clear portrait photo (JPG, PNG, or WEBP).
  2. Select Ratio & Crop: Choose 35×45 mm Passport or enter custom dimensions. Adjust the cropping rectangle to center the face and shoulders, then click "Confirm Crop".
  3. Edit & Background: Click "Auto Remove BG" to isolate the portrait. Select a formal background color (White, Light Blue, Blue, or Custom Color) and add a thin black border stroke (1–2px) if desired.
  4. Enhance Tone: Use the Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, and Warmth sliders or click "Magic Auto Enhance" to fix underexposed lighting.
  5. Export Single or Sheet: Download a single high-resolution JPG/transparent PNG, or click "Print Multiple Photos" to export a full 4×6 inch (typically 8 photos) or A4 layout ready for photo printers.

100% Client-Side In-Browser Execution: Photo Studio Pro processes your photographs strictly in your device's browser memory (HTML5 Canvas & local WebAssembly). ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Photo Studio)

What is the standard passport photo dimension in India?
The most common standard dimensions for Indian competitive exam portals, driving licenses, and official documentation is 35 mm width by 45 mm height with the face covering roughly 70–80% of the vertical frame.
How does background removal work without uploading my photo?
We use modern WebAssembly (WASM) neural network models (@imgly/background-removal) that execute directly inside your browser using your computer's local CPU/GPU capabilities.
How many passport photos fit on a 4×6 inch photo sheet?
At 35×45mm with 2mm cutting gaps, a standard 4×6 inch (102×152mm) sheet typically fits 8 passport photos arranged in a 2×4 grid.
Why is there a black border around the photo?
A thin border (stroke) makes it much easier to cut printed photographs straight using scissors or paper trimmers. You can adjust or turn off the border using the Photo Border (Stroke) slider.
Can I use this tool on a mobile phone?
Yes. The cropping and background replacement tools are fully touch-compatible on modern mobile browsers.
